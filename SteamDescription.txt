Small fixes for vanilla prisoner handling. More fixes may be added over time.

[h2]Wardens respect food policy during mental breaks[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] Vanilla [i]Pawn_FoodRestrictionTracker.GetCurrentRespectedRestriction[/i] returns no policy whenever the eater is in a mental state, no matter who is fetching the food. That makes sense for a breaking pawn feeding itself, but it also means a warden delivering food to a prisoner in a mental break ignores the prisoner's food policy and may bring fine meals, ambrosia or anything else you restricted.

[b]Fix:[/b] A Harmony postfix keeps the food policy when the food is fetched by another pawn (warden deliveries, feeding a patient). A pawn in a mental break that feeds itself still ignores its policy, as in vanilla.
